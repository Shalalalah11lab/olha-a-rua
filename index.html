About

Fabiola García Viveros is an art historian, photographer and engineer based in Mexico City. She trained in art history at Casa Lamm and at the Instituto de Investigaciones Estéticas of UNAM.

Her work moves between analog street photography, writing and research. She reads an image by describing the technique, then the image, then what form, idea and content do together.
